# Private by default

A new profile, its trophies, its playtime and its activity are visible to friends only. Messages from people who are not friends wait in a request inbox with their links inactive, and a new account may not write to strangers during its first day. Each player can open parts of their profile to everyone. Many players are teenagers. The GDPR asks for data protection by default (Article 25), and the Digital Services Act asks platforms that minors use for a high level of privacy and safety (Article 28); a default that starts open would leave each young player to find and close every setting.

## Considered options

- Public by default, as many gaming networks do: profiles easier to find, but every young player exposed until they act.
- Asking every player at sign-up: one more screen before the first game, and a default still has to be chosen for those who skip it.
